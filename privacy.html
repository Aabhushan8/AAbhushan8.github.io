Privacy policy

App: yt auto. Last updated: 10 October 2026.

Who this applies to

yt auto is a personal tool run by its developer. The only Google account that signs in to it is the developer's own. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the developer's permission, the app accesses the developer's own YouTube account to:

  • upload videos to the developer's channel;
  • read basic information about that channel and its videos;
  • read analytics for that channel, such as views and watch time.

What the app stores

The app stores an authorization token for the developer's account on the developer's own computer, with file permissions that restrict it to the developer. It also stores records of the clips it has made and uploaded, and the channel statistics it reads. This data stays on the developer's own computers.

What the app shares

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from uploading or reading channel data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
