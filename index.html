yt auto

A personal tool that prepares short educational video clips and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What the app does

yt auto finds videos that are licensed for reuse, cuts short clips from them, and uploads those clips to a single YouTube channel owned by the developer. Each clip credits the original channel, title, link and license.

What access it asks for

The app asks for permission to upload videos to, and read basic channel and analytics information from, the developer's own YouTube account. It is used only by its developer. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other people.

Your data

The app does not collect or share data about other people. See the privacy policy for details.
